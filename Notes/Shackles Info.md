# Shackles Info



## Difficulties:

##### Brain Freeze (Easy):

Enemies and bosses do 25% less damage, and have 25% less health.

Their detection boxes are smaller, and move slower.

Enemies spawn less commonly, but drop more loot.

Side Bosses do not spawn.



##### Headache (Normal):

Enemies and bosses do normal damage, and have normal health.

They have normal-sized detection boxes, and move at a normal pace.

Enemies spawn at a regular rate, and drop a normal amount of loot.

Side bosses spawn, but they give you 2 warning before confirming the fight.



##### Migraine (Hard):

Enemies and Bosses do 12% more damage, and have 12% more health.

Their detection boxes are slightly larger, and more a little faster.

Enemies spawn more commonly, but drop a normal amount of loot.

Items at shops and markets cost 25% more.

Side bosses only give you 1 warning before confirming the fight.



##### Cluster (Super Hard):

Enemies and Bosses do 25% more damage, and have 25% more health.

Their detection boxes are noticeably larger, and move faster.

Enemies spawn plentifully, and drop less loot, but rarer loot

Items that appear at shops and markets are rarer.

Items at shops and markets cost 50% more.

Side bosses don't give you a warning, except a sign at the entrance of their domain that says that it is a side boss and is optional.



##### Thunderclap (Insanely Hard):

Enemies and Bosses do 50% more damage, and have 50% more health.

Their detection boxes are doubled, and move 75% faster.

Enemies spawn way too much, and drop no loot, except occasionally a rare item.

Items that appear at shops and markets are very rare.

Items at shops and markets cost 100% more.

Side bosses are mandatory.

The save file is deleted if you die, instead of having 3 lives.



## Bosses:

### Main Bosses:

##### Stage 1 - The Master Swordsman

A stronger, faster, and larger swordsman. 75 HP.



##### Stage 3 - The Phantom Archer

A larger, faster archer that will teleport away if you get too close.

Shoots weakening (-20% Damage) arrows on <25% HP. 75 HP.



##### Stage 5 - The Champion Horse Jockey

A significantly faster horse jockey with high knockback.

Has a stomping attack with high damage. 100 HP.



##### Stage 8 - The Master Swordsman ***{CORRUPTED}***

An incredibly powerful Master Swordsman, that can propel high-knockback shock waves, giving you the vulnerable (Takes 20% more damage) effect.

Sword gets larger and more powerful. 200 HP.



##### Stage 10 - The Dragon

A massive flying enemy with 3 stages: Stage 1 (50-100% HP), Stage 2 (25-50% HP), and Stage 3 (0-25% HP).

Stage 1- Shoots large fireballs that explode.

Stage 2- Shoots faster fireballs with larger incendiary explosions.

Stage 3- Shoots beams of fire, dealing consistent damage, whilst still shooting fireballs, although less.

In stage 2 and 3, has a roar that deals lots of knockback. 200 HP.



##### Stage 12 - The Phantom Archer ***{CORRUPTED}***

An incredibly fast-shooting Phantom Archer that can create clones of itself, saving it from damage until its clones are destroyed.

Always shoots weakening (-33% Damage) arrows, but shoots 3 arrows at a time when on <25% HP. 200HP



##### Stage 15 - The Champion Horse Jockey ***{CORRUPTED}***

An incredibly fast Champion Horse Jockey that gets faster and stronger the more damage it takes.

Can jump onto high platforms, and swiftly fall down from them, heavily increasing the power and knockback of stomping attack. 300HP



##### Stage 18 - The Dragon ***{CORRUPTED}***

Becomes a 3-headed hydra, still with the 3 stages.

Red Head shoots Fireballs (25-100% HP) / Fire Beams (0-25% HP).

Blue Head shoots powerful slowing (-20% movement speed) Ice Crystals (25-100% HP) / freezing Ice Beams (0-25% HP).

Black Head does the roar (regardless of stage), and chomps you (0-50% HP), preventing attacking for 2-5 seconds. 300 HP.



##### Final Stage (Stage 20) - The Corruption

An all-powerful being that incorporates all former bosses.

Wields a super strong Corruption Sword with high knockback, damage, and swing verticality.

Can shoot super-fast weakening (-42% damage) arrows, and can teleport when damaged.

Rides a super fast and high jumping horse with a very dangerous stomp attack.

The horse is 3-Headed, 1 head shooting fire beams, another shooting ice beams, and the last shooting dangerous balls of corruption.

Can spawn super strong variants of corrupted enemies, and even bosses.



### Side Bosses:

##### Stage 2 - The Bandit

Moves insanely fast.

When attacking, steals some of you money, giving you vulnerability (20%).

Deals more damage the more money stolen. 150 HP.

###### Loot:

80% of money stolen

$0-500

Robbery Charm (Gets 20% more money from killing enemies)



##### Stage 4 - The Grappler

Uses a grapple gun to move from wall to wall, swing off roofs, and grapple to the player, damaging them.

Grapple can be hit by the sword to return to The Grappler.

If hooked by the grapple, you can move around to make The Grappler hit a wall, taking sums of damage.

Takes only 10% sword damage.

Upon smashing into a wall, the grappler is stunned for 5 seconds, also taking normal damage from the sword whilst stunned. 200 HP.

###### Loot:

$100-300

Grapple Gun



##### Stage 6 - Naval Captain

Has a water-based arena.

Rides a fast boat.

Shoots fast-moving harpoons to damage you and pull you in.

Also shoots super-fast explosive torpedos that stun you, do lots of damage, and deal lots of knockback. 250 HP.

###### Loot:

$120-350

Lucky Charm (Increases item luck)



##### Stage 7 - Elemental Mage

Shoots mysterious beams that will combust, stun, freeze, smite, or explode you.

Every 50 damage taken, it teleports to the next room, also increasing attack damage and speed. 300 HP.

###### Loot:

$150-400

Magic Tunic (Halves duration of negative effects on the wielder.)

##### Stage 14 (Boss 1) - Pyromancer

Every 24 Seconds - Casts a Firenado that bounces off walls.

Every 60 Seconds - Summons 10 fireballs that orbit around the Pyromancer, protecting it from damage until all 10 are destroyed.

Reaching 1/8HP, 1/4HP, 1/2HP, and 3/4HP - Shoots 5 Fireball Meteors into the sky, and then fall down without warning, exploding on impact. 400 HP.

$500

##### Stage 14 (Boss 2) - Cryomancer

Every 15 Seconds - Summons a row of Icicles above you that fall down, skewering you to the ground.

Every 24 Seconds - Casts a Blizzard for 12 seconds, that slows you down by 33% and decreases your visibility (Opacity: 33%)

Every 90 Seconds - Creates an Ice Castle with Snowball-Firing Cannons and 100HP. This Castle protects the Cryomancer.

Every 50 damage taken - Summons an Ice Monster that flings balls of ice and can stomp to make ice spikes suddenly emerge out of the ground.

Reaching 1/8HP, 1/4HP, 1/2HP, 3/4HP - Shoots a beam out of either side of the Cryomancer, doing **TONS** of damage, freezing you for 8 seconds, and supressing item effects for 20 seconds. 400 HP.

###### Loot:

$500

Hybrid Key (Unlocks Hybridmancer Side-Boss)



##### Stage 14 (Boss 3) - Hybridmancer

Every 15 Seconds - Shoots 3 slow-moving homing fireballs or summons a row of Icicles above you that fall down, skewering you to the ground.

Every 24 Seconds - Casts a Firenado that bounces off walls or casts a Blizzard for 12 seconds, that slows you down by 33% and decreases your visibility (Opacity: 33%)

Every 60 Seconds - Summons 10 fireballs that orbit around the Pyromancer, protecting it from damage until all 10 are destroyed or creates an Ice Castle with Snowball-Firing Cannons and 100HP. This Castle protects the Cryomancer.

Every 50 damage taken - Summons a Fire Dragon that flies around, shooting fireballs with high knockback or summons an Ice Monster that flings balls of ice and can stomp to make ice spikes suddenly emerge out of the ground.

Reaching 1/8HP, 1/4HP, 1/2HP, 3/4HP - Disappears, and summons a 100HP Pyromancer and a 100HP Cryomancer.

Upon death - Casts all pyromancer and Cryomancer spells at once, forcing you to survive them for 15 seconds to successfully win the fight. 800 HP.

###### Loot:

$1000

Do-or-Die Gem (When you would die to an attack, you fully recover, and deal 50 damage to all nearby enemies, 2-Use)

Flame Coat (Holder is immune to catching on fire)

Frost Coat (Holder is immune to freezing)



##### Stage 16 - Axe Thrower ***{CORRUPTED}***

Thrown axes smite if the blade hits you.

When on <1/2 HP: chases after you, swinging his axes at an alarming rate.

During this, axes are immune to being hit by your sword. 350 HP.

##### Stage 17 - Archer Troupe

10 masterfully trained archers that do an arrow rain every time an archer is killed.

When there's 5 or less archers left, 2 of those archers man ballistae on either side of the arena.

Once you damage an archer, the other archers cannot be harmed until you kill the attacked archer. 50 each.

###### Loot:

$500

Arrow Shield (Deflects arrows when held)



##### Stage 19 - Corruption Amalgamation

Combines all side-bosses.

Moves super fast and inflicts vulnerability.

Has a grapple gun to crash into you and takes 1/4 damage from your sword.

Shoots dangerous torpedos.

Teleports to next arena \& gets faster and stronger after taking 100 damage.

On 1/2 HP activates all Pyromancer and Cryomancer spells.

Upon taking damage shoots 5 arrows into the sky.

When on 90% HP, 70% HP, 30% HP, and 10% HP, corruptions portals open and shoot beams of corruption, doing **TONS** of damage. 1000 HP

###### Loot:

$1000
